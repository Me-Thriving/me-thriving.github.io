About Me-Thriving

Nursing insight. A preventive perspective.

Health is personal, and the factors that shape it are often connected. Me-Thriving brings a nursing perspective to preventive wellness, helping people explore their everyday health patterns and identify practical opportunities for positive change.

With nursing experience dating back to 1998, including case management across adult and pediatric care, I bring a foundation in patient-centered care and care coordination to my wellness work.

Our Philosophy

Small, informed steps can make a meaningful difference.

Wellness is not about perfection or following a one-size-fits-all plan. It is about understanding your own circumstances, recognizing patterns, and making thoughtful choices you can sustain.

Our approach emphasizes evidence-informed education, practical lifestyle strategies, and care for the whole person.

A note about care: Me-Thriving provides preventive wellness education and consulting. It does not replace medical diagnosis, treatment, or care from your licensed healthcare provider. Recommendations are tailored to the individual's needs and remain within the appropriate professional scope.
